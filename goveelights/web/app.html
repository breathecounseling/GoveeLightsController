Location (for sunset)

Frame rate

10-25 looked smooth in testing; 30 stuttered.

Colour curve (gamma)

1.0 = off. Raise it (around 2.2) if mid colours look washed out or orange looks yellow on the bulbs.

Devices

Bulb counts, landmarks and identify are in the diagnostics. Opening it pauses the effects.

Run in the background

On the PC, run python -m goveelights install-startup once. The app then starts silently at login, and you open this page from your phone as usual. uninstall-startup undoes it.
