Playlists

A show is a sequence of presets with transitions between them: a wipe across the house, a rise up the gable, a flash… It takes over, then hands back to whatever was playing.

Showtimes

Run a show automatically, e.g. every hour on the hour from 5 to 10 pm. Showtimes only play while the lights are on (schedule or Live).

Countdowns & events

At the moment itself the house counts down (digits on the zigzag, eaves filling up), then plays a celebration. "Days to go" shows the number on the zigzag for a few seconds every 15 minutes while the lights are on.

Game day

Celebrates when your team scores (touchdowns, field goals, goals, runs) and wins, in the team's colours. Scores come from ESPN's public scoreboard. TV is usually 20–60 s behind, so add a delay to avoid spoilers.

Weather

Adds weather on top of whatever is playing, using the forecast for your location (Open-Meteo, checked every 10 minutes).

Visitor page (QR sign)

Neighbours scan a sign in your yard and pick from presets you choose here. Their pick plays for a while, then the lights go back to normal. They can't see or change anything else. The link has a secret code; "New link" makes old signs stop working.

Map the bulbs with your camera

Makes 2D effects (ripples, sweeps, pictures, fire rising) line up with the real roofline. The lights blink a code, you film them for ~25 seconds, and your phone works out where every bulb is. Nothing is uploaded.
