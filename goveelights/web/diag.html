Is the white diode reachable in realtime?

Compare these by eye at one bulb. A: realtime RGB full white (likely RGB diodes only, slightly blue or pink). B: realtime warm mix. C: the official colorwc at 2700 K, which probably uses the W diode and may exit realtime. D: speculative RGBW (4-byte) frame. Rejected or garbled means unsupported.

E: decisive RGBW test

Sends 4-byte colours of pure red (255,0,0,0). Look at a few bulbs up close. If every bulb is red, the box reads 4 bytes per bulb, so the W channel is real. If bulbs cycle red, green, blue, off, it is reading the bytes as RGB, so D's "white" was just mixed colours.

If all red: tap D again and look closely. Is it a warm or neutral white (the W diode) rather than a bluish RGB white?

C takes the box out of realtime mode. The next A, B, D or E button turns realtime back on by itself (expect a blink).
